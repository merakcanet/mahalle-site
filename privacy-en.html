Privacy Policy — Mahalle

Effective date: 18 November 2026 · Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains how information is handled when you play Mahalle ("the Game"), a mobile game developed by merakcanet ("we", "us"). Players in Türkiye can also read the KVKK notice (in Turkish).

In short

  • The Game has no accounts. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos.
  • Your save game is kept only on your device. We have no servers and cannot see your save.
  • Rewarded ads are optional: one plays only when you tap a button to get a bonus. Apart from those, an occasional full-screen ad may appear at a natural break in the Game (travelling between shops, the ferry): never in the first minutes, minutes apart and with a daily limit. With the No-Ads Pack there are no ads at all.
  • Personalised ads are shown only if you allow them. If you don't, the Game is exactly the same.
  • Purchases go through Google Play (the App Store on iPhone); we never see your payment details.
  • The whole Game can be finished for free; purchases are completely optional.

1. Information stored on your device

So that you can keep playing, the Game stores the following on your device only: your progress (coins, shops, staff, tasks, achievements), your settings (language, sound, accessibility), the name you give your shop, a record of the packs you bought (for example the No-Ads Pack) and your ad preference.

None of this is sent to us. To delete it, use Settings → Reset progress in the Game, or uninstall the Game.

If your phone's own backup is turned on (Android backup or iCloud), your save may be included in your phone's backup, so you can continue on a new phone. That backup is a Google or Apple service under their rules; we cannot access it.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

In the mobile app, ads are served by Google AdMob. A rewarded ad starts only when you tap a reward button (for example "Get 2x"). In addition, an occasional full-screen ad (an interstitial) may appear at a natural break in the Game (travelling between shops, the ferry); never in the first minutes or the tutorial, minutes apart, with a limit per session and per day. The ad software starts with the first ad.

To serve ads, Google's advertising software may collect the following from your device and send it to Google:

  • your IP address (from which an approximate location, such as country or city, can be derived),
  • advertising identifiers (the Android advertising ID; on iPhone the IDFA only if you allow it) and the app set ID,
  • device and app information (device model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (an ad being shown, watched or tapped),
  • diagnostics (ad loading times, error and crash logs).

This information is used to show and measure ads, limit how often the same ad appears, prevent fraud and invalid clicks, and for statistics. Google processes it under its own policy: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Personalised ads: by default the Game asks for non-personalised ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks you; elsewhere the Game's own "Ad preferences" window asks you. Personalised ads are shown only if you agree. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad preferences. Even with non-personalised ads, Google may use some of the information above for frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement.

To delete or reset your advertising ID: on Android, Settings → Google → Ads; on iPhone, *Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking*.

If you own the No-Ads Pack, no ads are shown at all (neither rewarded nor interstitial), the bonuses are given straight away and the ad software is not started.

3. Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play Billing (on iPhone, the Apple App Store). We never see your card or payment details; Google or Apple processes the payment. We only receive the store's confirmation that a purchase was completed and the ID of the product bought, which we use to deliver the item and to bring it back with Restore purchases when you change phones. This record is kept in the save on your device.

For refunds and receipts, please use Google Play or Apple support. Their privacy policies: Google · Apple.

Parents can require approval for purchases with the device's parental controls (Google Family Link, Apple Screen Time).

4. Photo mode

Pictures you take in photo mode are saved to your device only when you tap Save. They are never uploaded.

5. Services the Game connects to

Apart from the ad and purchase services above, the Game does not send information to any server. We do not use analytics or crash-reporting tools. If we add one in the future, this policy will be updated first.

6. Children

The Game's content is suitable for everyone, but the Game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are limited to family-friendly content.

7. Retention and security

Your save stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the Game. Information collected by Google and Apple is kept under their retention rules. Communication with the ad and purchase services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

8. International transfers

Google and Apple may process information on servers in different countries, including the United States, using their own safeguards and in line with applicable law (such as the GDPR in Europe and the KVKK in Türkiye).

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe, UK GDPR, the KVKK in Türkiye or US state privacy laws), you have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we keep no data about you on any server, you can delete all of your Game data from your device yourself. You can manage what Google uses for ads in My Ad Center. For any question, contact us at the address below. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Changes

If we update this policy, we will change the date at the top; important changes will also be announced in the Game.
